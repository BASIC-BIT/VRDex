Mobile playback test

Test the live basicbit HLS relay, or compare prerecorded samples.

Ready

  1. Start a test. For video, enter Picture in picture.
  2. Switch apps and check that sound continues.
  3. Lock your phone for 15–30 minutes and listen.
  4. Return here and save the log before starting another test.
Logs stay on this device until you save them. The page cannot tell whether you heard sound while the phone was locked. HLS samples end after 35 minutes. Live video requires basicbit to be broadcasting.
